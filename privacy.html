Privacy policy

Privacy at 2.4 Recruit Ready

Effective 16 September 2026

2.4 Recruit Ready is provided by Winslo Consulting. It is an independent training app for people preparing for the New Zealand Police entrance fitness test through either the 2.4 km Run or Full Police PAT pathway. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or an official app of New Zealand Police.

Information stored by the app

The app stores your nickname, selected sex and BMI scoring categories, training pathway, height and weight, training-plan choices, 2.4 km times, optional press-up, vertical-jump and combined-grip measurements and their history, completed sessions, audio choices and plan start date locally on your device. Winslo Consulting does not receive this training information, and the app does not require an account or login.

Advertising and consent

The Android app uses Google AdMob to display advertising and Google's User Messaging Platform to collect and manage privacy choices where required. Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect and share an IP address (which may be used to estimate approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security and compliance. Advertising personalisation depends on your choices and applicable law, and data sent by Google's advertising software is encrypted in transit.

When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Advertising privacy choices appears directly below Privacy policy in the Training guide settings. Google's handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps.

You can disable ads free of charge under Guide, Help and support. This choice is stored on your device and can be changed there later. When disabled, the app stops requesting and showing banner and interstitial ads. Disabling ads does not delete data already held by advertising providers.

Sharing and sale of personal information

Winslo Consulting does not sell your locally stored training information. Advertising data may be processed by Google and participating advertising partners as described above.

Retention and deletion

Your training information remains on your device until you erase it using Reset training data in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices.

External links

If you choose Send feedback, the app opens an email draft containing the app version, Android version and device manufacturer/model. You can review or remove these details before sending to jameswc.work@gmail.com. Nothing is sent automatically. Rating and sharing open the Play listing or Android Sharesheet only when you choose those actions.

The app links to the official New Zealand Police recruitment website so you can check current requirements. External websites operate under their own privacy policies.

If you choose Support app, the app opens a hosted PayPal payment page in your browser. Ad removal is free and takes effect before that page opens. A contribution grants no additional app features. PayPal and the receiving business process any payment under their own terms and privacy policies; the app does not collect payment credentials or verify a payment.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are below the minimum age required to consent to digital services in your location without a parent or guardian.

Health information

The app provides general training guidance and is not medical advice. Stop exercising and seek appropriate professional advice if you are injured, unwell or concerned about symptoms.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app or its services change. For privacy questions, contact Winslo Consulting at jameswc.work@gmail.com.
